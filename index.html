Curious? Your privacy Privacy Policy Effective date: 28 October 2026 Curious? is an Android app offering interesting facts, short stories and historical events. You can use Curious? without creating an account. This policy explains what information is stored on your device, what information may be processed by third-party services and what choices you have. 1. Who to contact For questions about this policy or privacy in Curious?, contact the app publisher at info@curious.org.uk. 2. Accounts and information you provide Curious? does not require registration, a username or a password. The app does not ask you to provide your name, postal address or payment details to read its content. 3. Information saved on your device Curious? stores certain information locally on your device to make the app work, such as saved facts and stories, reading preferences and positions where supported, downloaded or cached content, and daily reminder settings. This information is used to provide the features you select. The app does not currently offer an online account that synchronises these preferences to a publisher-operated server. You can remove saved items within the app. You can also clear the app's storage or uninstall the app using Android settings; clearing storage may permanently remove local preferences, favourites and cached content. 4. Advertising and Google AdMob Curious? includes Google AdMob technology for advertising. When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, device identifiers (including an advertising identifier where available and permitted), app interactions, ad interactions and diagnostic information. This may be used to deliver or measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain services. Such information can be processed by Google and relevant partners rather than collected directly by the Curious? publisher. Advertising and consent handling depend on your region, device settings and choices. Where required, Curious? uses Google's consent-management technology to ask for advertising choices. You can refuse optional advertising consent and, where available, revisit your choices through Topics → Settings → advertising privacy choices. Refusing personalised advertising does not necessarily prevent all non-personalised or limited advertising where permitted. Further information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information in advertising. 5. Historical information and external services The Today in History feature retrieves information from Wikimedia services. When your device connects to these services, the service provider may receive normal technical request information, such as your IP address and request details, under its own privacy practices. Previously loaded information may remain accessible from the app's local cache. For more information, read the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy. If you choose to open an external source link, your browser or the destination website may process data according to its own privacy policy. 6. Notifications Daily reminders are optional and turned off by default. If you enable them, Curious? saves your chosen reminder time on your device and uses Android's local notification functionality. You can change the time or disable reminders in the app's Settings. You may also control notifications through Android settings. The daily reminder feature does not require an online Curious? account. 7. Purposes, retention and deletion Local information is retained on your device while needed to support your app preferences and cached reading experience, unless you clear it or remove the app. Information processed by Google AdMob, Wikimedia or other external services is governed by those providers' applicable policies and retention practices. We do not operate a Curious? user-account database from which to delete an account. For questions about data handling or deletion requests, contact info@curious.org.uk. For locally stored app data, use the in-app controls or Android's clear-storage/uninstall options. For information processed by a third party, its privacy controls may also apply. 8. Security Curious? uses local device storage for app preferences and standard network connections for online features and advertising. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your Android device and the app updated to help protect your information. 9. Children Curious? is a general-interest educational and entertainment app. It is not specifically designed as a service for children, and it does not provide child accounts. The app's applicable Google Play target-audience and advertising settings will determine any additional safeguards for younger users. 10. Your choices and rights You can use the app without registering, control optional reminders, manage saved content, change advertising privacy choices where provided, and adjust your device's privacy settings. Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on certain personal information. Contact info@curious.org.uk to make an enquiry. We may direct you to the relevant third-party provider when it controls the information concerned. 11. Changes to this policy We may update this policy when app features, advertising arrangements or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available at this page, with an updated effective date where appropriate. Curious? · Privacy Policy · Contact: info@curious.org.uk
